# Team Agreement — Team Orbit (fictional example)

## 1. Team identity
Team Orbit · Project: SeatFinder · Members: Noor A., Yousef K., Dana M., Hamad S. (fictional)

## 2. Team leader
Noor A. leads planning, runs weekly meetings, and is the contact for the supervisor. Leadership is reviewed at the start of CpE-495.

## 3. Communication

| Channel | Used for | Expected response time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Microsoft Teams (team channel) | Supervisor communication, official posts | Same day |
| WhatsApp group | Day-to-day coordination | 4 hours on weekdays |
| GitHub issues and pull requests | All project work and reviews | Review within 48 hours |

## 4. Meetings
Weekly, Sunday 1:00–1:45 pm, library group room or Teams. Minutes in `meetings/` within 24 hours; the note-taker rotates alphabetically.

## 5. Definition of Done
A task is done when: the acceptance criteria in the issue are met, a pull request was reviewed by one other member, files are in the right `docs/` folder as `.docx` + `.pdf`, and the board card is moved to Done.

## 6. Decisions
Small decisions: owner decides and notes them in the issue. Major decisions (architecture, components, scope): Decision Record, discussed at a meeting, majority vote; the leader breaks ties.

## 7. Conflict resolution

| Possible source of conflict | How we will resolve it |
| --- | --- |
| Uneven workload | Review the board every meeting; reassign issues openly |
| Missed deadlines | Tell the team 48 hours ahead; second miss goes to the supervisor |
| Disagreement on technical direction | Write a short DR with options; decide at the next meeting |

## 8. Responsibilities of the team to each member
Share credit by name in the report, give feedback on the work and not the person, and help a member who is stuck before the deadline.
